# Antislop Audit 001 (2026-10-21)

Scope: Live visitor counter implementation and SEO domain update (`src/components/Footer.jsx`, `src/App.jsx`, `index.html`, `public/robots.txt`, `public/sitemap.xml`).

## Findings

1. **[HIGH | Hard Gate R-03]** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (lines 101-123, 133-168): Footer contact links (`mailto:`, `tel:`) and Quick Links have ~20px vertical height without touch padding, falling below the 44px minimum mobile tap target.
2. **[HIGH | Hard Gate R-32]** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (lines 87, 102, 115, 133, 178, 191, 204): Footer links and social icon buttons lack an explicit, high-contrast `:focus-visible` indicator verified across light and dark modes.
3. **[MEDIUM | Purpose-Gate R-10]** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (line 81): Full-bleed `backdrop-blur-xl` on the footer background adds unmotivated glassmorphism that conflicts with `DESIGN.md` ("Crisp, subtle hairline borders... No blurry glassmorphism") and R-10 dose caps.
4. **[LOW | Quality Lock R-05]** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (lines 75-77, 130-170): Splitting 5 Quick Links into a 3-column sub-grid inside a 4-column footer cramps link columns on tablet widths (`768px-1024px`).
5. **[LOW | Quality Lock R-31 & Code Comment Skill]** `src/components/Footer.jsx` (lines 85, 97, 127, 173, 218) & `index.html` (line 52): JSX comments (`{/* Branding */}`, `{/* Contact */}`, `{/* Quick Links */}`, `{/* Social */}`, `{/* Bottom bar */}`) restate the immediate heading/tag, and `index.html` line 52 contains an em dash in the comment.

---

## Resolution Report (Approved: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

- **Finding 1 (FIXED):** Added `min-h-[44px] inline-flex items-center` to the branding link, Contact links (`mailto:`, `tel:`), and all Quick Links in `src/components/Footer.jsx`.
- **Finding 2 (FIXED):** Added `focusRingClass` (`focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-emerald-600/400 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`) to every interactive link and social icon button in `src/components/Footer.jsx`.
- **Finding 3 (FIXED):** Replaced `backdrop-blur-xl` overlay with solid substrate colors (`bg-[#f8fafc]` light, `bg-[#0b0f17]` dark) and a crisp `border-t` hairline divider per `DESIGN.md`.
- **Finding 4 (FIXED):** Replaced the 3-column nested slice in Quick Links with a 2-column `<nav aria-label="Footer navigation" className="grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-6 gap-y-1">` layout, and added intermediate `sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4` breakpoints so tablet widths reflow cleanly.
- **Finding 5 (FIXED):** Removed redundant JSX section-header comments in `src/components/Footer.jsx` and replaced the em dash in `index.html` line 52 comment with a colon.
